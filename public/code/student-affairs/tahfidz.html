Tahfidz Al-Qur'an Program

Memorizing & Understanding the Quran

About Tahfidz Program

The Tahfidz Al-Qur'an program is a specialized initiative designed to help students memorize and deeply understand the Qur'an. This program combines traditional memorization methods with modern teaching techniques and spiritual guidance.

Our experienced Qur'an instructors provide individualized guidance to help each student progress at their own pace. The program fosters discipline, concentration, and spiritual development while building a strong community of learners.

We believe that memorizing the Qur'an is not just about memorization, but also about understanding its meanings and applying its teachings in daily life.

Program Features

Experienced Instructors

Qualified Qur'an teachers with years of experience in Tahfidz education and Islamic knowledge.

Structured Curriculum

Well-designed learning path with clear goals and milestones for progression.

Small Group Sessions

Personalized attention through small group classes ensuring individual progress tracking.

Certification

Recognition and certification for students who complete memorization milestones.

Spiritual Guidance

Religious and spiritual mentorship to develop Islamic understanding and values.

Annual Competitions

Tahfidz competitions and recitation contests to motivate and celebrate achievements.

Memorization Levels

Beginner

Target: 1-5 Juz (chapters)

Foundation building with focus on proper pronunciation (Tajweed) and memorization techniques.

Intermediate

Target: 6-15 Juz

Building upon foundation with increased memorization and understanding of meanings.

Advanced (Hafiz/Hafizah)

Target: 30 Juz (Complete Qur'an)

Complete memorization of the entire Qur'an with mastery of Tajweed.
