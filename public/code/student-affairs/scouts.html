Scouts Program

Developing Leaders & Citizens

About Our Scouts Program

Our school's Scouting program is part of a worldwide movement dedicated to developing young people as active citizens and responsible members of society. We provide youth with opportunities to discover their potential and contribute to society in a meaningful way.

Through exciting outdoor adventures, practical skills training, and community service, our scouts learn leadership, teamwork, and environmental stewardship while having fun and making lasting friendships.

We are proud to be part of the global scout family, following the established scout law and promise to develop character, courage, and commitment.

Scout Divisions

Cub Scouts

Ages: 10-13 years

Focus: Fun and adventure with basic outdoor skills

  • Basic camping skills
  • Teamwork exercises
  • Community service
  • Badge earning
Boy/Girl Scouts

Ages: 13-17 years

Focus: Adventure, leadership, and personal growth

  • Advanced camping & hiking
  • Leadership training
  • Environmental projects
  • Merit badges
Venturers

Ages: 17+ years

Focus: Challenge and service leadership

  • Expedition planning
  • Leadership roles
  • Community service
  • Personal challenges

Scout Activities & Events

Camping Expeditions

Regular camping trips to develop outdoor skills and bonding among scouts.

Community Service

Environmental projects, disaster relief, and community welfare initiatives.

Adventure Training

Rock climbing, rappelling, kayaking, and other adventure sports.

Scout Jamborees

Large gatherings with scouts from other schools and regions for friendship and learning.
