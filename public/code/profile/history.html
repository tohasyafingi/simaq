School History

Our Journey of Excellence

Our Rich History

Founded in 1995

SMA School was established with a vision to provide quality education and develop well-rounded individuals. Over the past three decades, we have grown from a small institution to one of the most respected schools in the region.

Major Milestones

  • 1995: School founded with 200 students and 15 faculty members
  • 2001: Achieved accreditation and expanded campus facilities
  • 2008: Introduced advanced science and technology programs
  • 2015: Established international exchange partnerships
  • 2021: Implemented modern digital learning infrastructure

Academic Excellence

Over the years, SMA School has consistently maintained high academic standards and has produced thousands of successful graduates. Our alumni have gone on to pursue careers in medicine, engineering, law, business, and various other fields, making significant contributions to society.

Growth and Expansion

Today, we serve over 1,200 students with a dedicated faculty of 80+ experienced educators. Our campus spans across 5 acres with state-of-the-art facilities including science laboratories, computer labs, sports fields, and a modern library.
