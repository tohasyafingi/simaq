Our diverse extracurricular programs help students develop leadership skills, explore new interests, and build lasting friendships.

Sports & Athletics

Basketball

Join our championship basketball team. Practice teamwork, strategy, and athletic excellence.

Soccer

Develop skills and sportsmanship on our competitive soccer team with experienced coaches.

Swimming

Train in our Olympic-size pool with certified instructors and compete at regional levels.

Music Band

Express yourself through music. Join our band and perform at school events and competitions.

Drama Club

Develop acting skills and confidence through theatrical performances and workshops.

Art Club

Explore various art forms including painting, sculpture, and digital art with guidance from professional artists.

Robotics Club

Build and program robots. Compete in national robotics competitions and learn cutting-edge technology.

Debate Team

Sharpen critical thinking and public speaking skills through competitive debates and discussions.

Science Club

Conduct experiments, participate in science fairs, and explore the wonders of scientific discovery.

Community & Leadership

Volunteer Club

Make a difference in the community through service projects and charitable initiatives.

Environmental Club

Promote sustainability and environmental awareness through green initiatives and projects.

Yearbook Committee

Capture memories and develop journalism skills by creating our annual yearbook publication.
