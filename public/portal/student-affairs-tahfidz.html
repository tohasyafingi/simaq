Tahfidz Program

Quranic Memorization & Islamic Studies

About Tahfidz Program

Our Tahfidz program provides students with the opportunity to memorize the Holy Quran while developing strong Islamic character and values. Under the guidance of qualified instructors, students progress at their own pace in a supportive environment.

Structured Curriculum

Systematic approach to Quran memorization with proven methods

Qualified Teachers

Experienced instructors with Ijazah certification

Flexible Schedule

Daily sessions that fit with academic schedule

Certification

Recognition upon completion of memorization milestones

Program Structure

  • Daily Sessions: Morning and afternoon memorization classes
  • Tajweed Training: Proper pronunciation and recitation rules
  • Review System: Regular revision to maintain memorization
  • Islamic Studies: Understanding Quranic meanings and context
  • Progress Tracking: Individual monitoring and parent updates

Program Levels

Beginner Level

Juz 30 (Juz Amma)

Foundation level focusing on short surahs and basic tajweed rules. Perfect for students starting their memorization journey.

Intermediate Level

5-15 Juz

Progressive memorization with emphasis on retention and understanding. Students develop consistent study habits.

Advanced Level

15-30 Juz (Complete Quran)

Complete memorization with advanced tajweed and preparation for Ijazah certification.

Benefits of Tahfidz Program

Spiritual Growth

Deepen connection with the Quran and strengthen faith through memorization and understanding.

Memory Enhancement

Improve cognitive abilities and memory retention skills that benefit all areas of study.

Character Building

Develop discipline, patience, and perseverance through consistent practice and dedication.

Community Connection

Join a supportive community of students and teachers dedicated to Quranic learning.
