Student Leadership & Representation

About Student Council

The Student Council (OSIS - Organisasi Siswa Intra Sekolah) is the representative body of our student body. It serves as a bridge between students and school administration, advocating for student rights and promoting school spirit.

Our council is dedicated to organizing school events, improving the student experience, and fostering a positive school culture. Members are elected by their peers and demonstrate exceptional leadership, integrity, and commitment to the school community.

We believe in empowering students to take active roles in shaping their educational experience and contributing to a vibrant school environment.
