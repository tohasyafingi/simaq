Scouts Program

Building Character Through Adventure

About Our Scouts Program

Our Scouts program develops leadership, teamwork, and outdoor skills through exciting activities and community service. Students learn valuable life skills while having fun and making lasting friendships.

Outdoor Adventures

Camping, hiking, and wilderness survival skills

Leadership Training

Develop leadership and teamwork abilities

Merit Badges

Earn badges for skill development

Community Service

Give back through service projects

Our Activities

  • Weekly Meetings: Regular training sessions and skill development
  • Camping Trips: Quarterly outdoor camping experiences
  • Community Projects: Environmental cleanups and charity work
  • Competitions: Participate in regional and national scout competitions
  • Skill Workshops: First aid, navigation, survival skills, and more

Skills You'll Learn

Survival Skills

Fire building, shelter construction, water purification, and wilderness navigation.

First Aid

Emergency response, CPR, wound care, and basic medical knowledge.

Leadership

Team management, decision making, communication, and responsibility.
