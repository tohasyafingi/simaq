About This Book

A comprehensive guide to advanced physics concepts, specifically designed for high school students preparing for college-level coursework and competitive examinations.

This textbook provides an in-depth exploration of classical mechanics and thermodynamics, two fundamental pillars of physics. Written by Dr. Robert Chen, a renowned physics educator with over 20 years of teaching experience, this book bridges the gap between high school physics and university-level understanding.

What You'll Learn

The book is structured to build understanding progressively, starting with fundamental concepts and advancing to complex applications:

Part I: Classical Mechanics (Chapters 1-12)

  • Kinematics: Motion in one, two, and three dimensions, including projectile motion and circular motion
  • Newton's Laws: Comprehensive treatment of forces, mass, and acceleration with real-world applications
  • Work and Energy: Conservation of energy, potential and kinetic energy, power
  • Momentum: Linear and angular momentum, collisions, center of mass
  • Rotational Motion: Torque, moment of inertia, angular velocity and acceleration
  • Gravitation: Universal gravitation, orbital mechanics, Kepler's laws
  • Oscillations: Simple harmonic motion, pendulums, damped and driven oscillations
  • Waves: Wave properties, interference, standing waves, sound waves

Part II: Thermodynamics (Chapters 13-20)

  • Temperature and Heat: Thermal equilibrium, heat transfer mechanisms, specific heat
  • Kinetic Theory: Molecular interpretation of temperature, ideal gas law
  • First Law of Thermodynamics: Internal energy, work, heat, thermodynamic processes
  • Second Law of Thermodynamics: Entropy, heat engines, Carnot cycle, refrigerators
  • Phase Transitions: States of matter, latent heat, phase diagrams
  • Real Gases: Van der Waals equation, critical point

Key Features

Clear Explanations

Complex concepts broken down into understandable segments with step-by-step derivations and intuitive explanations.

Worked Examples

Over 200 fully solved problems demonstrating problem-solving techniques and application of concepts.

Practice Problems

More than 500 end-of-chapter problems ranging from basic to challenging, with answers provided.

Visual Learning

Hundreds of diagrams, graphs, and illustrations to enhance understanding of abstract concepts.

Who Should Read This Book

This textbook is ideal for:

  • High school students in advanced physics courses (AP Physics, IB Physics HL)
  • Students preparing for physics olympiads and competitive examinations
  • Self-learners seeking to deepen their understanding of physics
  • First-year university students reviewing fundamental concepts
  • Teachers looking for comprehensive teaching resources

Prerequisites

To get the most out of this book, students should have:

  • Solid understanding of algebra and trigonometry
  • Basic calculus knowledge (derivatives and integrals) - though calculus concepts are reviewed where needed
  • Completion of introductory physics course or equivalent
