Annual School Festival 2024

November 5, 2024 | Posted by Admin

Overview

The Annual School Festival is one of the most anticipated events of the school year. This year's festival promises to be bigger and better than ever, showcasing the talents of our diverse student body and celebrating the vibrant culture of SMA School.

Festival Highlights

  • Cultural Performances: Traditional and modern dance performances, musical concerts, and theatrical productions
  • Art Exhibitions: Display of student artwork including paintings, sculptures, and digital art
  • Academic Showcase: Science fair projects, technology demonstrations, and academic competitions
  • Food Festival: Culinary creations from students and parent volunteers
  • Sports Demonstrations: Performances by school sports teams and athletic exhibitions

Event Details

Date: November 10-12, 2024
Time: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Location: SMA School Campus
Admission: Free for all visitors

How to Participate

Students from all grades are encouraged to participate in the festival. Whether you want to perform, exhibit artwork, participate in competitions, or help organize, there's something for everyone. Interested participants should contact their class representatives or the Student Council for more information.

Important Notes

  • All performances must be family-friendly and appropriate for school setting
  • Artwork submissions must be received by November 1, 2024
  • Science fair projects must follow school guidelines
  • Parking will be available for parent visitors

For more information, contact the Student Council or visit the school office. We look forward to seeing you at this year's Annual School Festival!
