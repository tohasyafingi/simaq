Admission Information 2024-2025

Registration Period

Start: January 15, 2025

End: March 31, 2025

Selection Tests

Date: April 15-20, 2025

Location: School Campus

Results Announcement

Date: May 10, 2025

Method: Online Portal

Requirements

Document Requirements

  • Original birth certificate or certified copy
  • Latest school report card (Junior High)
  • Copy of national ID (student & parents)
  • Family card copy
  • Passport-sized photos (4x6 cm)
  • Medical examination report

Academic Requirements

  • Completion of Junior High School (Grade 9)
  • Minimum GPA: 7.0 from previous school
  • Passing score on entrance examination
  • Passing interview
  • Good conduct and discipline record

Admission Process

1
Registration

Submit application form with required documents

2
Selection Test

Take entrance examination in Indonesian, English, Mathematics

3
Interview

Personal interview with school staff and teachers

4
Acceptance

Results announcement and enrollment confirmation

Tuition & Fees

Item Amount (per year)
Registration Fee $500
Tuition Fee $4,000
Academic Materials $300
Extracurricular Activities $200
Transportation (Optional) $1,000

Important Notes

  • Early registration is encouraged for better consideration
  • Scholarship opportunities are available for qualified candidates
  • Payment plans are available upon request
  • All documents must be original or officially certified
  • Falsification of documents will lead to disqualification
