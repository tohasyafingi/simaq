Admissions

Join our community of learners and future leaders

Admission Period 2025-2026

Applications are now open! Registration period: January 15 - March 31, 2025

Admission Process

1
Submit Application

Complete and submit the online application form with required documents

2
Entrance Test

Take the entrance examination covering core subjects

3
Interview

Attend an interview with school administrators and counselors

4
Enrollment

Receive acceptance letter and complete enrollment procedures

Requirements

Completed Application Form

Fill out all sections of the application form accurately and completely

Recent Photographs

4 passport-sized color photographs (3x4 cm) taken within the last 3 months

Academic Records

Certified copies of report cards from previous school (last 2 years)

Birth Certificate

Original and photocopy of birth certificate

Health Certificate

Medical examination report from certified healthcare provider

Parent/Guardian Information

Identification documents and contact information of parents or legal guardians
